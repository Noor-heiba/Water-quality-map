Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Information We Collect

Water Quality App does not collect any personal information. When you search a ZIP code, the location data is used only to retrieve water quality information and is not stored or transmitted to our servers.

Third Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • OpenStreetMap - for map display
  • OpenMeteo - for climate and soil data
  • EPA Water Quality Portal - for water quality data
  • Nominatim - for ZIP code geocoding

Data Storage

No personal data is stored on our servers. All water quality data is sourced from public EPA databases.
