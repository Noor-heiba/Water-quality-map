Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 9, 2026

Overview

AquaIQ does not collect any personal information. We are committed to protecting your privacy while providing accurate water quality data for your area.

Information We Collect

When you search a ZIP code, the location data is used only to retrieve water quality information and is not stored or transmitted to our servers. No personal data is collected, stored, or shared.

Third Party Services

  • OpenStreetMap — map display
  • OpenMeteo — climate and soil data
  • EPA Water Quality Portal — water quality data
  • Nominatim — ZIP code geocoding
  • Google Earth Engine — satellite imagery analysis

Data Sources

All water quality data is sourced from the US EPA Water Quality Portal — a public database with 38,979 monitoring locations across all 50 states. No proprietary or private data is used.
