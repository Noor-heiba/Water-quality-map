How to Use

Enter any US ZIP code in the search bar to instantly get water quality information for your area. The app uses 38,979 EPA monitoring locations plus AI predictions for areas without direct measurements.

Water Quality Index (WQI)

The WQI is calculated using the NSF formula, the most research-backed water quality index used by scientists worldwide.

90-100: Excellent
70-89: Good
50-69: Fair
0-49: Poor

Parameters Explained

pHAcidity/alkalinity (ideal: 6.5-8.5)
TurbidityWater clarity in NTU (ideal: <1)
TDSTotal dissolved solids mg/L (ideal: <500)
HardnessCalcium/magnesium mg/L
ArsenicToxic metal (limit: 0.01 mg/L)
NitrateAgricultural runoff (limit: 10 mg/L)
IronCauses staining (limit: 0.3 mg/L)
FluorideDental health (limit: 1.5 mg/L)

Data Sources

Water quality data comes from the EPA Water Quality Portal. For ZIP codes without nearby EPA monitoring, our ML model predicts water quality using 11 features including satellite imagery (Sentinel-2), soil data (OpenMeteo), elevation, and distance to coast.
