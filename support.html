Understanding the Water Quality Index

The Water Quality Index (WQI) is calculated using the NSF formula based on pH, turbidity, TDS, hardness, dissolved oxygen, nitrate, fluoride, arsenic and temperature.

  • 90-100: Excellent
  • 70-89: Good
  • 50-69: Fair
  • 25-49: Poor
  • 0-24: Very Poor

Data Sources

Water quality data is sourced from the EPA Water Quality Portal with 38,979 monitoring locations across all 50 states.
